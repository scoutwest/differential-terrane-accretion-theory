   Lithosphere >= 150 km covers 1.182e+08 km2 (23.2% of the Earth): 24% under cratons, 53% under other continental/transitional provinces, 23% outside all provinces (ocean or unmapped).

LIMITS
   The thickness grid is a model (Afonso et al. 2019: joint inversion of seismic, gravity, elevation, heat-flow and petrological data).
   Some of its structure may follow the surface geology it was constrained by, so the match may be partly built in; a second, independent
   model (for example LITHO1.0) has not been run. No ages are used (P1). Grid stride 0.4 deg; sampled up to 3,000 points per craton;
   the offset search is a flat-earth shift, adequate to a few degrees but not at high latitude; the null moves each craton as a rigid shape.
   'Thick keel' thresholds follow P9's 150-250 km; the mapping from lithosphere thickness to keel is the model's, not a measurement of a chemical keel.
